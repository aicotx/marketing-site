Agent Deployment

Long-running agents on the workflows your marketplace already standardized — inbox triage, queue watching, scheduled jobs, drafting for approval. Trust is engineered, not assumed: every agent starts in draft-only mode and graduates to act-with-approval, then act-and-report, one workflow at a time.

Observability comes with it — logs, cost tracking, and escalation paths treated as seriously as for any other production system. New agents ship as plugins into the marketplace you already run, so capability keeps compounding in one place.

Fractional AI Engineer

A senior AI engineer on your team for a fixed number of days each month — embedded, in your environment, under your priorities. For companies that have real AI work most months but not forty hours a week of it, and no appetite for a recruiting war over a hire the market has already priced out of reach.

This is how the ladder keeps moving between engagements: pilots get built, the catalog stays healthy, agents stay supervised, and your team levels up by working next to someone who ships. Scope rides the roadmap the audit produced — nothing freelance about it — and when a climb is big enough to deserve its own fixed scope, it becomes one of the engagements above instead of quietly eating the days.
